AuroraPro · Typeface Study Nº 001

One name, written in light, three ways.

The AuroraPro wordmark currently rents whatever UI font is loaded — Inter today, Space Grotesk if a user flips a setting. These are three custom letterform studies for a wordmark face called AuroraPro: the same nine letters drawn on one shared skeleton (cap 190, x-height 110), styled three different ways. All three keep the brand's existing fixtures — the violet→cyan ramp, the night sky, the play-circle mark.

#A855F7 #7C3AED #22D3EE #0A0E1E night metrics — cap 190 / x-ht 110 / stroke 24–36
CONCEPT 01

Borealis the ribbon of light

The aurora as motion — soft, continuous, friendly. A rounded geometric monoline where the wave lives inside the letters: the A’s crossbar is a ribbon mid-flutter, and every r releases its arm upward like a streamer.

Construction
Geometric monoline — circular bowls, single-storey a
Terminals
Round, with rising light-trail flicks on r
Signature move
Aurora-wave crossbar on the A; gradient ink runs the word
Wears best on
App header, onboarding, README — the friendly face

Scale test: holds to nav-bar size; the wave reads as a glint before it disappears. In production, flatten ink to solid violet below ~24 px.

CONCEPT 02

Meridian signage for your own infrastructure

What AuroraPro is under the paint: one Go binary, systemd, Postgres — yours. Every curve is milled to a 45° chamfer, the apex is a flat-topped survey marker, and the o is the play-mark aperture cut octagonal. Keeps today’s AURORA / PRO two-tone split.

Construction
Octagonal — 45° chamfers in place of curves
Terminals
Butt-cut, engineered; no softening anywhere
Signature move
Flat-top A + milled o; cyan Pro against cool-white Aurora
Wears best on
CLI banners, status page, version badges — the operator’s cut

Scale test: the sharpest small-size performer — chamfers rasterize cleanly, almost pixel-font at 12 px. Least “entertainment” of the three, most “tool.”

CONCEPT 03

Photon the title card

For the ten-foot screen. A heavyweight rounded display cut by two full-width light bands — an auroral curtain sheared horizontal — with the glow leaking through the cuts. The vertical ramp runs green→cyan→violet, the actual color order of an aurora, shifted onto the brand.

Construction
Heavy monoline (stroke 36) with stencil cuts
Terminals
Round, blunt — mass over detail
Signature move
Two light bands through the whole word; under-glow leaks through
Wears best on
Splash screen, TV UI, launch poster — the marquee cut

Scale test: a display face by design — below ~40 px the bands close up. Pair it with Borealis or Meridian for everything smaller.

Recommendation: lead with 01 · Borealis. It carries the aurora story in the letterforms themselves, keeps the existing gradient, and is the only concept that works everywhere the name appears today — header, README banner, PWA icons.

If you want a family rather than a single face: Borealis as the wordmark, Photon as the splash/TV display cut (same skeleton, so they harmonize), and Meridian’s chamfer language reserved for ops surfaces like the status page.

From mockup to font file

  1. Pick a direction (or a two-cut family) and I’ll refine spacing and details.
  2. Extend the winner beyond these nine letters: full lower/upper case, digits, punctuation.
  3. Trace the outlines in FontForge (free, scriptable) and export AuroraPro.woff2.
  4. Ship via @font-face in static/redesign/app/src/index.css; swap the header lockup in App.tsx and the README banner to the new face.

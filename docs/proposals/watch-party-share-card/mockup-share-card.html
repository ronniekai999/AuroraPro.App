The Watch-Party Share Card

Schedule a party, get a poster. One tap turns a booked watch party into a downloadable PNG built for a phone screen — the artwork, the moment, the host, and a code that gets a friend into the room. Rendered server-side so the same image also unfurls when the link is pasted anywhere.

Story format — the one people post

1080×1920, sized for Instagram and Snapchat Stories, TikTok, and phone wallpapers. Every measurement below is the real output pixel value, so this page doubles as the renderer's layout spec.

Why it reads

The poster is the hook

566×850 of real key art at the optical centre, lit from behind by the aurora curtain. At thumbnail size a viewer recognises the title before they read a word.

The time is the second anchor

118px gradient display type. Legible at 15% scale — which is exactly how big it is in a crowded story tray.

The code is the conversion

A QR nobody has to type, plus a six-character fallback for people watching the story on the phone they'd have to scan with.

Two more crops from the same composition

Same layers, same tokens, reflowed. The square is what people put in a feed or drop into Discord; the wide card is the one Aurora emits as og:image so a pasted party link unfurls into artwork instead of a bare URL.
